About This System
Database Schema
  • PERSON - Base entity for players & referees
  • TEAM - National teams & confederations
  • PLAYER - Player details (inherits PERSON)
  • REFEREE - Match officials (inherits PERSON)
  • STADIUM - Venue information
  • MATCH - Match fixtures & results
  • MATCH_EVENT - Goals, cards, substitutions
  • PLAYER_MATCH_STATS - Player positions & shirt numbers
Features
  • CRUD operations on all tables
  • Foreign keys shown as names

  • Stored procedures & functions (Stage 4)
